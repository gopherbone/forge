- **2026-11-15 04:52:21** 🧭 `agent` — Chosen: A-variant clothespin topology, no crossing. Upper lever = upper jaw + forehead bridge + short back tab (thumb pad); frame = lower jaw + bottom bridge + belly/legs/tail (the handle). They join only through ONE spring hinge in a window behind the mouth corner, which is both pivot and spring. The hinge legs run VERTICAL, parallel to the squeeze force, so the bending moment (F x horizontal distance) is near-uniform along them. To fit ~26 mm of strip in a low head it is folded as a 3-leg meander (odd count: top bridge -> bottom bridge). forge flex hinge L26 t1.7 19 deg: 1.08% (util 0.54); budget x1.3 for the leg farthest from the thumb line -> ~0.70.

- **2026-11-15 04:52:22** 🧭 `agent` — Sizing from the clamp window first (no preload, so clamp ~ proportional to jaw deflection d+interlock): hinge k = E b t^3/(12L) = 1460*8*1.7^3/(12*26) ~ 184 N.mm/rad. Roll seat at 26 mm from the pivot: F = k(d+i)/a^2 -> O4 ~1.2 N, O8 ~2.3 N (1-4 N). Squeeze: M_stop ~ 184*0.32 ~ 59 N.mm, thumb at b~10 mm behind the pivot -> ~6 N with ~3.2 mm stroke. Thumb 40 mm from the jaw tips (R7). E is uncertain 2x (1460 vs 2780 MPa): forces scale with E, strain does not, so aim at the low end of both force windows.
